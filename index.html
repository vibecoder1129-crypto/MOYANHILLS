About MOYEN HILLS

MOYEN HILLS is a clothing brand from Latvia. The first piece is a single t-shirt: boxy, cut short at the bottom, and screen printed on the front, the back and the neck label.

It is made in Latvia and pre-made in small numbers. When it is gone, it is gone, so follow along for the next one.

Questions

How does it fit?

Boxy, with short sleeves and a cut-off style bottom, so it sits wide and a little short. For exact measurements, email us before you order.

When does it ship?

Every tee is pre-made, so please allow 1–3 days for order fulfilling. You get an email when it is on its way.

What is it made of?

100% cotton, with custom screen printed graphics on the front, the back and the neck label. Made in Latvia.

How do I pay?

Place your order from the cart. We confirm it by email with the payment details and the total including delivery.
