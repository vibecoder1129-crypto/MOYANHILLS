What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

  • Orders: your name, email, phone (optional), delivery address, the items, any note and any gift message (which we print and put in the parcel). We use them to confirm, deliver and support your order (to perform our contract with you) and keep order records because accounting and tax law require it (legal obligation).
  • Cancellations: if you use Cancel an order, your name, email, order number and the items, to handle your withdrawal (legal obligation).
  • Accounts (only if you create one): your email, password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), name, phone, address and your orders, so you can see your orders and check out faster (contract).
  • “Notify me when it’s back”: if you ask to hear when a sold-out size returns, your email, the product and size, and your language, to send you that one email (your request; legitimate interest). We send nothing else to that address.
  • Reviews and photos (only if you write one from your account): your rating, review text, the name you give and your photo, plus a link to your account and the order. We check each review first; once approved, the rating, text, name and photo are public on the product page (your consent, which you can withdraw any time). We redraw photos before upload, which removes hidden details such as where they were taken.
  • “Join the Hills family”: your email address, to send you news about drops (your consent). We first email you a link to confirm it is really you (double opt-in). You can withdraw consent any time with the unsubscribe link in every email, or by writing to us.
  • Abuse protection: when you place an order or try a promo code, our database keeps a one-way, salted fingerprint of your internet address for up to 24 hours, to limit fake orders and code guessing (our legitimate interest in keeping the shop safe). The address itself is not stored by us.
  • Visitor counts: to see how many people visit the shop, each page view is counted anonymously: the page, where the visit came from (for example “Instagram” or a website name), the device type (phone, tablet or computer) and a one-way code made from a secret, the date, your internet address and browser type. The code changes every day and cannot be turned back into your address, so we can’t recognise you or follow you from one day to the next. No cookies are used and nothing is stored on your device. Counts are kept for 13 months (our legitimate interest in understanding how the shop is used). Browsers set to “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control are not counted.
  • Website hosting: our host processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure (legitimate interest).

We do not use your data for automated decisions or profiling, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Orders and cancellations: for the period Latvian accounting and tax law requires, 5 years after the end of the year of the order, then deleted.
  • Account: until you delete it (you can do this yourself on the Account page). Your orders then stay only as order records, no longer linked to an account.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. We keep a record of when you signed up and unsubscribed, to show we had your consent.
  • “Notify me” requests: until we send the email, then deleted 30 days later. Write to us to remove one sooner.
  • Reviews and photos: until you or we delete them. Deleting your account deletes them, and they are included in “Download my data”.
  • Abuse-protection fingerprints: 24 hours.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. If you have an account, you can download your data and delete your account yourself on the Account page. Otherwise email moyenhills@gmail.com; we reply within one month. You can also complain to the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (dvi.gov.lv) or the data protection authority where you live.

How we protect it

The site runs only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each customer see only their own orders and details. Only named staff, signing in with a password and a second code from an authenticator app, can see orders, and every admin change is logged.

Children

Our shop is not aimed at children under 16. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we tell account holders by email.
